In one line: Card Club Arcade has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no payments. Your game data stays on your own device. Room-code games connect you directly to the other players.

What is stored on your device

Your display name, avatar, a random player tag, the friend names you add, favourite games, theme and card-back choices, sound and display settings, your play-coin balance, daily streak, missions and achievements, and your match history. This is kept in your browser or app storage on your device only. It is not sent to us and is not synced to any account.

Room-code multiplayer

When you create or join a room, the app uses the public PeerJS signalling service and WebRTC to connect your device to the other players. To do this, the signalling service and the other players in the room can see technical connection details such as your IP address, and the other players receive your display name and the game moves needed to play. Public relay (STUN/TURN) servers operated by third parties may be used to help the connection. We do not run these servers and do not receive this data.

What we do not do

  • No advertising and no ad identifiers.
  • No analytics, tracking or crash-reporting services.
  • No accounts, sign-in or payments. Play coins are a game feature only: they have no cash value and cannot be bought or sold.
  • We do not sell or share personal data.
