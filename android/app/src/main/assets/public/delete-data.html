There is no online account to delete. Card Club Arcade keeps your information only on your own device, so you control it.

Remove your data in the app

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Choose Clear player data and confirm.

This removes your name, avatar, friend names, favourites, coins, missions, achievements and match history from this device.

Remove everything including the app

Uninstall the app, or clear the site/app storage in your phone settings (Android: Settings → Apps → Card Club Arcade → Storage → Clear data).

Room games

Room games are peer-to-peer and are not stored on a server by us. Once a room closes, no room data is kept by the app.
