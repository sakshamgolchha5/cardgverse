## Full description (max 4000)

Card Club Arcade is a friendly collection of 15 classic card games in one app. Learn a new game in a minute with the illustrated how-to-play, then play against smart bots, pass one phone around the table, or invite friends with a simple room code.

THE GAMES
Trick-taking: Callbreak, Hearts, Court Piece, Dehla Pakad, 3-2-5, Confidence (Oh Hell)
Shedding: Seven, President, Donkey, Yaniv, 13-card Rummy
Party & memory: Bluff, Ghulam Chor, Spoons, Cambrio

PLAY YOUR WAY
- Solo against bots on Easy, Medium or Hard
- Pass & Play on one phone, with a hand-over screen so nobody peeks
- Room Code: friends join from their own phones
- Invite friends or recent players with one tap (WhatsApp or any app)
- Always landscape, built for phones, works offline for bot games

MADE FOR LARGE TABLES
Many games support up to 10 players, with one or more decks.

EARN AND UNLOCK
- Win coins for every match, a daily check-in and daily missions
- Build a daily streak and unlock achievements
- Spend coins on table themes and card backs
- Need a hand? Spend coins on a hint from the strongest bot

FAIR AND SIMPLE
No ads. No accounts. No real-money gambling. Coins are play money and cannot be bought. Your data stays on your device.

Rules are shown in the app for every game, and you can change options such as number of players, decks, rounds and rule variations before you start.
